Native Chromium Speed

Powered by the Blink rendering engine and V8 JavaScript engine. Experience lightning-fast page execution, WebGL rendering, and fluid video streaming.

Isolated Tab Sandboxing

Each browser tab runs in its own native `WebContentsView` with OS-level sandboxing, context isolation, and zero Node.js access for maximum security.

🎯

Smart Omnibox

Unified address bar and search engine with auto-protocol prefixing, instant domain resolution, and one-click bookmarking.

Hardware-Accelerated Tabs

Open, close, and switch between dozens of tabs smoothly without page reloading or UI freezing.

🔒

Private & Local Data

Your bookmarks, settings, and browsing history remain 100% on your local computer. No telemetry and no mandatory cloud accounts.
