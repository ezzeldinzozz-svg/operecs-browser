October 9, 2026
v0.20.0

20 changes

New and improved19

  • History moved into a fast database, with full-text search on the History page
  • Keep history for a chosen time, or clear history, downloads, cookies or cache whenever Operecs quits
  • Interrupted downloads continue where they stopped, even after quitting
  • A warning before a form on a secure page sends your data without encryption; the lock shows when a secure page loads anything insecurely
  • Closing a window or quitting asks first when a page has unsaved changes
  • Reloading a page you got by sending a form asks before sending it again (it used to do nothing)
  • Look Up, Speech and Emoji & Symbols in the right-click menu; Paste as Plain Text (Cmd+Shift+V)
  • The Bookmarks menu lists your bookmarks and folders, and bookmarks show their site icons
  • Extensions can be turned off without removing them, and their toolbar buttons hidden
  • Proxy settings, clearer pages when you're offline or a site can't be found, and help signing in to hotel and café Wi-Fi
  • Sites can connect to USB, serial, HID and Bluetooth devices you pick
  • Import history from Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi or Arc, and export all your data
  • Customize the new tab page — A background color or your own image, pinned and custom shortcuts with site icons, and hide shortcuts or bookmarks
  • Address autofill — Save your addresses and fill name, email, phone and address fields with one click
  • Profiles — Keep work and personal browsing completely separate, each with its own color
  • Mute a whole site from the tab menu
  • Tracking parameters (utm_, fbclid, gclid…) are removed from addresses you open
  • The new tab page counts the ads and trackers Operecs blocked
  • Energy saver — On battery, tabs you haven't used for 10 minutes go to sleep

Security1

  • Weekly checks for Electron security fixes, and urgent fixes install within minutes
